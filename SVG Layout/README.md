# GoIndiaCab Partner - Screen & SVG Flow Index

This directory contains all 103 UI/UX vector screen designs for the **GoIndiaCab Partner (Driver) Application**, organized sequentially by user journey and feature module.

---

## 📋 Module Overview

| # | Module / Flow | Screens | Description |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| 01 | **Splash Screen** | 1 | App initial launch and branding display |
| 02 | **Auth Screen** | 4 | Mobile number login, OTP verification, resend, and error states |
| 03 | **Partner Registration** | 6 | Initial vehicle number entry and ownership OTP verification |
| 04 | **Vehicle Eligibility** | 4 | Vehicle eligibility checks and model qualification status |
| 05 | **Personal Details** | 8 | Driver profile, address, Driving Licence, Aadhaar KYC & Selfie verification |
| 06 | **Bank Verification** | 5 | PAN card details, bank account, IFSC validation, and KYC approval status |
| 07 | **Vehicle Documents** | 7 | Upload of RC, Insurance, Fitness, Commercial Permit, and Emission certs |
| 08 | **Vehicle Photo Instructions** | 9 | Vehicle exterior, interior, odometer photo capture and inspection review |
| 09 | **Payment Screen** | 6 | Partner registration/subscription fee overview, payment methods, UPI flow, and status |
| 10 | **Application Status & Training** | 5 | Application submission confirmation, review status tracking, approval/rejection, and driver training module |
| 11 | **Partner Home & Status** | 3 | Online/Offline switch, active radar map, and notifications hub |
| 12 | **Ride Requests & Management** | 14 | Ride dispatch cards, scheduled rides, navigation, toll capture, multiple stops, cancellation, and incident reporting |
| 13 | **Trip Execution Flow (Round Trip)** | 9 | Complete end-to-end active trip lifecycle (booking acceptance, navigation, pickup, in-transit, completion, and cashless fare settlement) |
| 14 | **Earnings & Settlements** | 9 | Earnings dashboard, daily/weekly breakdowns, performance incentives, bank settlement history, and tax reports |
| 15 | **Partner Profile & Vehicle** | 5 | Partner rating/reviews, profile editing, registered vehicle information, and referral incentives |
| 16 | **Help & Support** | 2 | Frequently asked questions, issue reporting, and in-app live chat support |
| 17 | **Settings & Account** | 6 | Application preferences, language switching, notification management, app info, logout, and account deletion |
